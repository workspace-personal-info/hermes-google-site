Hermes Workspace — Personal Integration

A personal integration connecting Hermes Agent with the Google services authorized by the account owner.

The integration uses Google APIs to access or manage Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts, based on granted permissions and the account owner’s requests. Available operations include reading and, when requested and authorized, editing data and sending messages.

Access enables the agent to carry out tasks requested by the account owner. This integration is intended for personal use.
