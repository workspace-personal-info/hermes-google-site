Privacy Policy

Hermes Workspace is a personal integration that connects Hermes Agent to the account owner’s Google account to carry out personal tasks requested by that owner.

Data and use

Depending on permissions granted and the owner’s requests, the integration can access Gmail messages and drafts, Calendar events, Drive files, documents, spreadsheets, and contacts. When requested and authorized, it can also create or modify data, share files, or send messages. Data is used to perform requested tasks and return results to the owner.

Services and retention

Requests to Google services are sent to Google APIs. Content processed by the agent may be sent to the model provider configured in Hermes and may be retained in conversation history, memory, or logs according to the configuration. Information about changes may be shown in the Hermes interface. Retention and handling depend on the services and settings in use.

Credentials are managed in the Hermes profile and are not published on this site. Data may be shared with recipients selected by the owner when an action such as sending a message or sharing a file is requested.

Control and revocation

The account owner can manage or revoke access through Google Account connections and manage Hermes settings and history. Revocation stops further access but does not automatically delete information already retained in histories or by other services.
